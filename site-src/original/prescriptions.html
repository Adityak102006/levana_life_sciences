    What we accept

    A valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner, with the doctor's name, signature and date. A clear photo taken with your phone is perfectly fine.

    2

    How verification works

    Our pharmacist matches each medicine on your prescription before dispatch. If we have a question, we call you and speak in plain language.

    3

    Your privacy matters

    Prescriptions are health data. They are stored in encrypted form, accessed only by our pharmacist, and deleted on request, in line with the DPDP Act.

    4

    What we never sell online

    Schedule X drugs, narcotics and habit-forming medicines are never sold through this website, in line with Indian regulations.

    Good to know

    Common questions about prescriptions.

    Which medicines need a prescription?
    Products marked "Prescription required" on our website need a valid prescription from a registered doctor. These include Schedule H and H1 medicines such as antibiotics. Everyday OTC products, vitamins, ayurveda and devices do not need one.
    How long does verification take?
    Usually within 30 minutes during store hours (8:00 AM to 11:00 PM). After verification we confirm your order on call or WhatsApp, and then it is packed and dispatched.
    Can I send my prescription on WhatsApp instead?
    Yes. Send a clear photo to +91 98765 43210 along with your name and delivery address, and our pharmacist will take care of the rest.

    Questions about your prescription?

    Our pharmacist is available daily from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM to help you.
